# Design Principles

Defaults for a user-facing flow, not evidence about users, and not a substitute for requirements or accessibility standards.

- Optimize the primary user goal: current state, next action, feedback, and recovery path.
- Keep choices and information proportional to the task; disclose secondary detail progressively.
- Preserve keyboard, screen-reader, focus, touch, and narrow-screen operation; apply current WCAG and platform requirements where they govern.
- Make loading, empty, error, disabled, permission, and success states explicit.
- Validate important flows with representative users or an observable proxy; visual polish is not usability evidence.

## Output

Target users and context, the primary flow, the evidence behind it, accessibility risks, and unresolved product decisions. A design-only task implements no UI.
