# Product Discovery

**When to use:** Starting a new product or feature area and need market grounding before discussing what to build. Trigger: "let's start a new product", "help me understand the market", "before we brainstorm", "what's out there for X".

**Goal:** A comprehensive discovery document saved to `docs/product/discovery.md` — descriptive enough that any agent starting fresh understands the product deeply without needing additional context. The SRS starts directly at requirements because this document covers everything else.

**Constraints:**
- Research actual products, not just describe what the user already knows.
- Prioritize products users would consider as real alternatives.
- Identify gaps and complaints, not just features.
- The document must stand alone: vision, principles, users, market, position, business model, assumptions — all in one place.

**Process:**
1. Clarify: what does this product do, who is it for, what problem does it solve.
2. Research similar products, competitors, and adjacent solutions: what they do, who they target, pricing, key weaknesses, user sentiment.
3. Map the landscape: main players, unmet needs, common complaints, success patterns.
4. Define position: where this product fits, ICP, what winning looks like.
5. Save to `docs/product/discovery.md`: What It Is / Problem / Target Users / Design Principles / Market / Gaps / Position / Business Model / Assumptions.

**Done when:** Document saved and approved. Suggest **brainstorming** with the discovery doc as input — no need to re-explain the product.
